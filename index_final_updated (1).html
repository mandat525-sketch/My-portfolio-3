Our Story

A Sanctuary in the Heart of Bangalore

Nestled in the vibrant Yellappa Layout of East Bangalore, Hotel Pallavi Grand offers a refined retreat for the discerning traveller. Whether visiting for business or leisure, our thoughtfully appointed rooms and warm hospitality promise an experience that lingers long after you leave.

Conveniently located near Manipal University, EPFO offices and major commercial corridors, we blend accessibility with comfort — making every stay effortless.
